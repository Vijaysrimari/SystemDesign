PhotoGram Design
System Design · LLD + HLD

Instagram-Style Photo & Short Video Platform

A scalable architecture for uploading photos and short videos, publishing posts, following users, liking, commenting and viewing personalized feeds for millions of users.

Problem statement

Design a scalable system that allows users to upload photos and short videos, create posts, follow users, like posts, comment, and view personalized feeds.

Functional requirements

Upload and validate media · Create and view posts · Follow and unfollow · Like and comment · Personalized feed · Push notifications

Non-functional requirements

Millions of users · Read-heavy traffic (feed reads far outnumber uploads) · Low-latency media delivery · High availability · Eventual consistency for counters and feeds
